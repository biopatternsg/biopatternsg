I. `expert_objects.txt`:
Contains symbols that identify objects within a molecular interaction network. Each line contains a symbol; synonymous symbols for it are also included, each on its own line (you will see one symbol per line). All symbols in `expert_objects.txt` may belong to one of the following categories: genes, gene variants, proteins, protein variants, diseases, small molecules (such as ligands), mRNA, motifs, or synthetic compounds.

II. `synonyms.pl`:
This is a general dictionary of identifiers in Prolog format, containing records that establish which identifiers may be synonymous with a primary identifier.

Example of a record in `synonyms.pl`:

`synonyms('<identifier>', ['<identifier>', '<synonym-1>', '<synonym-2>', '<synonym-3>',..., '<synonym-n>']).`

Where:

`<identifier>`: The primary identifier for the record.
Note: The primary identifier is always part of the list of objects and is always the first identifier in that list.

Note: The identifiers `<synonym-1>`, `<synonym-2>`, `<synonym-3>`,..., `<synonym-n>` are potential synonyms for the record's primary identifier; however, they may not actually be synonyms—in other words, errors may exist. Note: The entry `synonyms('<identifier>', ['<identifier>', '<synonym-1>', '<synonym-2>', '<synonym-3>',..., '<synonym-n>'])` is the primary entry for the `<identifier>` in question. III. aligned.pl:
Contains records indicating the alignment status of every symbol found in expert_objects.txt, relative to the records in synonyms.pl.

Definition: A symbol in expert_objects.txt is considered "aligned" if it appears as a primary identifier in any record within the synonyms.pl file.

The following are the possible reporting scenarios for a symbol from expert_objects.txt within aligned.pl:

A. If a symbol from expert_objects.txt appears as a primary identifier in any record within synonyms.pl, it will be reported in aligned.pl as follows:

aligned('symbol').

For example, if the object in expert_objects.txt is SST, it is reported in aligned.pl as:

aligned('SST').

B. If a symbol from expert_objects.txt also appears in records within synonyms.pl other than its primary record, then the primary identifiers of those other records may be synonyms for the symbol in expert_objects.txt. If this is the case, the symbol will have an entry in aligned.pl in the format: aligned_as('symbol', [...]). The structure of such an entry is as follows:

aligned_as('symbol', ['<identifier-1>', '<identifier-2>', '<identifier-3>',..., '<identifier-n>']).

Where:

['<identifier-1>', '<identifier-2>', '<identifier-3>',..., '<identifier-n>'] is a subset of the list of primary identifiers in synonyms.pl that may serve as synonyms for the symbol from expert_objects.txt currently being considered.

For example, if the identifier in expert_objects.txt is SST and it meets the criteria described in B, its entry in aligned.pl would be:

aligned_as('SST', ['SST', 'GROWTH HORMONE-SECRETING PITUITARY ADENOMA', 'SOFT TISSUE INJURIES']).

Note: If the symbol being processed already has an entry of the type `aligned('symbol')` in `aligned.pl`, then that symbol will also appear somewhere within the list `[...]` in an `aligned_as('symbol', [...])` entry; otherwise, it will not appear in such a list.

Note: A symbol from `expert_objects.txt` that lacks an `aligned('symbol')` entry in `aligned.pl` may still have an `aligned_as('symbol', [...])` entry. This means there may be alternative candidate symbols available, which could allow it to be aligned. In such a case, the original, unaligned symbol in `expert_objects.txt` will be replaced by the candidate symbol that was verified as a correct synonym. As a result, the original symbol will now appear as aligned in `aligned.pl`—specifically because the original identifier was replaced by the candidate symbol you verified as a correct synonym. You will learn the procedure for doing this later.

C. If a symbol in `expert_objects.txt` does not meet the criteria for A or B, it will be reported in `aligned.pl` as:

`no_aligned('symbol').`

For example, if the object in `expert_objects.txt` is `TATA`, it is reported directly as:

`no_aligned('TATA').`

D. A symbol in `expert_objects.txt` that is reported in `aligned.pl` as `no_aligned('symbol')` CANNOT have any `aligned('symbol')` or `aligned_as('symbol', [...])` entries in the `aligned.pl` file.
