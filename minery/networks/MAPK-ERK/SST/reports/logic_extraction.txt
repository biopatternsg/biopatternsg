6. LOGICAL RULES / INFERENCIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Rule R1 (bidirectional pairing): For most events E(A,r,B), the converse
event(E(B,r,A)) is also present. Verified count: event/3 facts are almost
entirely mirrored pairs.

Rule R2 (transitive correlation via expert objects): If OCTREOTIDE
negative_correlation AKT1 and OCTREOTIDE negative_correlation NEUROENDOCRINE
TUMORS, then AKT1 and NEUROENDOCRINE TUMORS share a regulator (OCTREOTIDE)
→ candidate co-regulation hypothesis.

Rule R3 (SSTR convergence): OCTREOTIDE associates with / negatively correlates
SSTR1, SSTR2, SSTR3, SSTR4, SSTR5; SSTR2 and SSTR5 associate with
NEUROENDOCRINE TUMORS → OCTREOTIDE→SSTR→NET axis.

Rule R4 (MAPK-ERK suppression by OCTREOTIDE): OCTREOTIDE negative_correlation
MAPK1 (line 2528) and MAPK3 (line 2530) → somatostatin analogue suppresses
ERK1/2 MAPK signaling.

Rule R5 (SST suppressive on NET): SST negative_correlation NEUROENDOCRINE
TUMORS (lines 2538, 2539) → endogenous somatostatin inversely linked to NET.

Rule R6 (synonym equivalence): Two event/3 facts using synonyms of the same
PubTator entity describe the same interaction (e.g. DCTN6 ≡ P27 ≡ CDKN1B;
S6K ≡ RPS6KB1; P53 ≡ TP53). False-synonym artifact: NEOPLASMS synonym list
contains 'MICA' meaning "microinvasive carcinoma" ≠ MICA gene (documented in
reference_reports/02_synonymous_interactions.txt — form reference only).
