Study details for genetic variants:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
40314039: Screening Biomarkers and Risk Factors for COVID-19 Progression in a
          Border Population Between Brazil-Bolivia. n=243. qPCR genotyping of 7
          targets. rs12979860 and rs2070788 showed significant association (P<0.05).

41153412: Genetic Variability in NKG2 Receptors and Their Ligands: Associations
          with SARS-CoV-2 Infection and COVID-19 Severity. qPCR of NKG2A, NKG2D,
          HLA-E, MICA/MICB SNPs. NKG2A rs7301582 T and HLA-E rs1264457 A alleles
          predominant in infected. MICA rs1051792 A in hospitalized (OR=14.95).

34116286: Variant-genetic and transcript-expression analysis showed a role for
          the chemokine-receptor CCR5 in COVID-19 severity. n=1,461. CCR5-Delta32
          (rs333) protective (OR=0.66, p=0.01). No homozygotes among patients.

35987511: Systematic review and meta-analysis of human genetic variants
          contributing to COVID-19 susceptibility and severity. 84 studies,
          130 polymorphisms in 61 genes. 7 susceptibility variants + 11 severity
          variants confirmed (ACE1, APOE, CCR5, IFITM3, ACE2, AGTR1, TNFA).

37342325: Genetic factors influencing COVID-19 in Sardinia: role of HLA-G.
          n=801. Neanderthal LZTFL1 rs35044562 detrimental (P=0.001).
          rs11549407 (HBB) protective (P=0.005). HLA-G 3'UTR Del/Del protective.

37499788: Adverse effect of VEGFR-2 (rs1870377) on clinical course of COVID-19
          in females and males in age-dependent manner. n=160. CCR5Delta32
          protective >=65 years. TLR3 rs5743313 protective in males <65.

39407172: Potential protective association of CXCR4 rs2228014 with COVID-19
          severity in adult Egyptians. n=300. AA genotype exclusive to mild/moderate
          cases suggesting protective role.

41993206: Reduced COVID-19 severity in Africa: systematic review. ACE2
          polymorphisms rs2285666, rs73635825 prevalent in Africans.

42139264: Impact of Surfactant Protein-D Gene Polymorphism on COVID-19 Clinical
          Outcomes. n=135. SP-D rs721917 TT genotype linked to increased severity.

40222985: GWAS of side effects after COVID-19 booster vaccination in Japan.
          n=2,554. rs76152249 (IL1RL1 locus) associated with lymph node swelling
          severity (P=1.46x10-9).

41947332: ACE2 Polymorphisms and COVID-19 Severity. n=332. rs140473595 CT/TT
          high risk of ICU (OR=7.22, p<0.0001). rs200180615 not significant.
