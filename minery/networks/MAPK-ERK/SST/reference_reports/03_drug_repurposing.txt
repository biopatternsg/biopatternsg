================================================================================
REPORT 3: COVID-19 DRUG REPURPOSING LANDSCAPE
COVID-19 Immunology Study (COVID-19-IMMUNOLOGY-50)
================================================================================

All drugs/compounds with negative_correlation to COVID-19 in kBase.pl
(negative_correlation = therapeutic/antagonistic effect reported in literature)

================================================================================
A. APPROVED ANTIVIRALS
================================================================================

  REMDESIVIR (RDV)
    - Prodrug nucleotide analog, targets RdRp (nsp12)
    - Also negative_correlation with CORONAVIRUS INFECTIONS, INFECTIONS

  FAVIPIRAVIR
    - RNA-dependent RNA polymerase inhibitor
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19

  UMIFENOVIR (ARBIDOL)
    - Broad-spectrum antiviral, membrane fusion inhibitor
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19

  MOLNUPIRAVIR
    - Oral nucleoside analog, induces viral error catastrophe
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19
    - Cotreatment with NIRMATRELVIR AND RITONAVIR DRUG COMBINATION

  NIRMATRELVIR AND RITONAVIR DRUG COMBINATION (PAXLOVID)
    - 3CL protease inhibitor + CYP3A4 booster
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19, KIDNEY DISEASES,
      LIVER FAILURE ACUTE, CARDIAC OUTPUT LOW

  4-OXIME URIDINE (NHC / BETA-D-N4-HYDROXYCYTIDINE)
    - Molnupiravir active metabolite
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19

================================================================================
B. JAK INHIBITORS
================================================================================

  BARICITINIB
    - JAK1/JAK2 inhibitor, FDA-approved for COVID-19
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19

  TOFACITINIB
    - JAK1/JAK3 inhibitor
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19, IL10, CD274, KLRK1, IFNG

  RUXOLITINIB
    - JAK1/JAK2 inhibitor
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19, IFNG, GLB1

  NEZULCITINIB (TD-0903)
    - Lung-selective JAK inhibitor
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19

================================================================================
C. CCR5 ANTAGONISTS
================================================================================

  MARAVIROC
    - CCR5 antagonist (HIV entry inhibitor)
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19, CCR5, CD274

  CENICRIVIROC (CVC)
    - CCR2/CCR5 dual antagonist
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19, CCR5, CCR2, INFLAMMATION,
      MULTIPLE ORGAN FAILURE, RESPIRATORY INSUFFICIENCY,
      NON-ALCOHOLIC FATTY LIVER DISEASE

  LERONLIMAB
    - CCR5 monoclonal antibody
    - Negative_correlation with POST-ACUTE COVID-19 SYNDROME, CCR5

================================================================================
D. IMMUNOMODULATORS / CORTICOSTEROIDS
================================================================================

  DEXAMETHASONE
    - Corticosteroid, RECOVERY trial standard of care
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19
    - Cotreatment with CENICRIVIROC

  HYDROXYCHLOROQUINE
    - Antimalarial, immunomodulatory
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19, IFNG, CD8A, CD4, CD69,
      CCR5, TIGIT, TNF, FAS, ITGB1, KLRB1, INFLAMMATION

  AZATHIOPRINE
    - Purine synthesis inhibitor, immunosuppressant
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19, AUTOIMMUNE DISEASES,
      COLITIS ULCERATIVE, HEPATITIS AUTOIMMUNE, JAUNDICE

  METHYLPREDNISOLONE / PREDNISOLONE
    - Corticosteroids
    - Negative_correlation with INFECTIONS

================================================================================
E. NATURAL COMPOUNDS / PHYTOCHEMICALS
================================================================================

  SAIKOSAPONIN D
    - Triterpene saponin from Bupleurum
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19
    - Association with CAT, IL6, CHEK1, NOX5, JAK3

  GLYCYROL
    - Licorice-derived compound
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19
    - Positive_correlation with CSF2, CXCL8, IFNG, CCR5

  LICOPYRANOCOUMARIN
    - Coumarin derivative from licorice
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19
    - Positive_correlation with CSF2, CXCL8
    - Association with CCR5, IFNG, TNF

  QUERCETIN
    - Flavonoid, found in many plants
    - Association with COVID-19 (context-dependent)

  BERBERINE
    - Isoquinoline alkaloid
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19

  KAEMPFEROL, HESPERIDIN
    - Flavonoids
    - Negative_correlation with COVID-19
