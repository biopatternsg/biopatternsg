5. NEOPLASMS / MICA (PubTator artifact -- WARNING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
synonyms.pl: synonyms('NEOPLASMS', [..., 'MICA', ...]).

  CRITICAL: In this context, 'MICA' in NEOPLASMS synonyms means
  "microinvasive carcinomas" -- NOT the MICA gene.

  This produces false matches like:
    event('NEOPLASMS',association,'AKT1')  =~ event('MICA',association,'AKT1')

  These are NOT true synonymous pairs. The MICA gene and the "MICA"
  abbreviation for "microinvasive carcinomas" are entirely different
  entities. However, because PubTator assigned 'MICA' to the NEOPLASMS
  synonym list, a subset of events (12 pairs) show this conflation.

  Impact: 12 confirmed false-synonym pairs. This artifact should be
  excluded from any deduplication effort.
